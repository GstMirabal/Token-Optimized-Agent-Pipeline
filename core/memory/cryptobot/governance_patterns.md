# CryptoBot Governance Patterns

## GP-026: The Planning Triad
- **Pattern**: For every significant architectural or functional change, the discourse must follow this immutable sequence:
    1. **Debate**: Identification of gaps and discussion of technical trade-offs.
    2. **Implementation Plan**: Detailed technical breakdown and file-by-file impact analysis.
    3. **Roadmap Initialization**: Once the plan is approved, the strategic Roadmap is updated or created to reflect the new state.
- **Rationale**: Ensures total constitutional alignment and prevents tactical drift before strategic sanctioning.
